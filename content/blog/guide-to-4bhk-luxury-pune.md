1. **Low Density:** True luxury is exclusivity. Seek projects with fewer apartments per floor to guarantee privacy.
2. **Cross Ventilation & Views:** A premium 4 BHK must offer unobstructed hill views and massive decks.
3. **Smart Home Automation:** Modern elite homes must integrate seamless tech for lighting, security, and climate control.
