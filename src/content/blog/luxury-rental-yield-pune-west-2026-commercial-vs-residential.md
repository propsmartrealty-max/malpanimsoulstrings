# Rental Yields in Pune West: Commercial Office Spaces vs Ultra-Luxury 4 BHKs

For high-net-worth investors in Pune evaluating where to deploy 3–5 Cr in real estate, the choice between **commercial office space** and **ultra-luxury residential** has never been more interesting. The conventional wisdom — that commercial always yields more — is being disrupted by structural shifts in both the office market and the premium rental housing market in Pune West.

## The Traditional Commercial Case

Commercial office space in Balewadi, Baner's commercial spine, and S.B. Road typically commands gross rental yields of **6–8% per annum** for Grade A spaces. Anchor tenants are typically mid-to-large IT and BFSI companies on 5–9 year leases with lock-in periods, providing reliable income stability.

The floor rates for commercial in this belt range from ₹65–110 per sq ft per month, with quality retail commanding up to ₹150+. At face value, these yields look compelling versus residential.

## The Luxury Residential Counter-Case

The premium rental market for [4 BHK luxury apartments](/4bhk-luxury-malpani-m-soulstrings-baner-pashan-link-road) in the [Baner Pashan Link Road corridor](/baner-real-estate-market-malpani-m-soulstrings-baner-pashan-link-road) has undergone a structural transformation since 2023. The primary demand drivers:

1. **C-Suite expat housing**: Multinational firms expanding in Pune (Siemens, Bosch, KPMG, Deutsche Bank) offer housing allowances of ₹1.2–2.5 lakh/month for their India-based global leaders
2. **Senior IT leadership**: CTOs, VPs, and business heads at large IT firms in Hinjewadi phases now prioritize luxury residential rentals as a lifestyle statement
3. **NRI Returnees**: Non-resident Indians returning for extended project assignments (6–24 months) demand turnkey luxury without ownership commitment

Commercial assets come with significant ongoing management complexity: lease negotiations, fit-out allowances, maintenance service charges, CAM (Common Area Maintenance) disputes, and potential vacancy risk during economic downturns.

Premium luxury residential, particularly in a low-density project like Malpani M SoulStrings, appeals to a profile of tenants (expat executives, NRI returnees) who treat the apartment with care, pay reliably, and require minimal intervention.
