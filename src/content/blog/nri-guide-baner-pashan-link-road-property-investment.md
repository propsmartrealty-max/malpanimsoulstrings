## Regulatory Framework for NRI Buyers
Under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), NRIs and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) are permitted to purchase residential property in India without prior approval from the Reserve Bank of India. Key regulatory points include:

- **Payment channels**: Purchases must be made through NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) accounts, or through inward remittance from abroad.
- **Repatriation**: Sale proceeds from up to two residential properties can be repatriated, subject to RBI guidelines and applicable tax provisions.
- **Power of Attorney**: NRI buyers can appoint a trusted family member or legal representative in India through a registered Power of Attorney to handle documentation, site visits, and registration formalities.
- **RERA protection**: All residential projects in Maharashtra must be registered under MahaRERA, providing NRI buyers with the same legal protections as domestic purchasers.

## Tax Considerations
NRI property buyers should be aware of:

- **TDS on rental income**: Tenants are required to deduct TDS at 30% on rental payments to NRI landlords (before applicable surcharge and cess).
- **Capital gains tax**: Long-term capital gains (for properties held more than 2 years) are taxed at 20% with indexation benefits. Short-term gains are taxed at the applicable slab rate.
- **DTAA benefits**: India has Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements with many countries, which can help NRIs avoid being taxed twice on the same income.
