## Architectural Anatomy of a Sky Villa

What distinguishes a standard apartment from a sky villa or penthouse? It comes down to structural column efficiency and orientation:

1. **100% Cardinal Alignment:** True Vastu-compliant layouts align exactly with the cardinal compass points. This guarantees that morning light enters the eastern deck, while western windward currents flow through the residence for natural cross-ventilation.
2. **Double-Height Decks:** Premium 3bhk luxury homes and 4bhk residences incorporate expansive, double-height private decks that serve as seamless extensions of the living room, offering panoramic views of the Sus-Baner hills.
3. **Column-Free Living Zones:** High-rise towers often have massive structural columns intruding into rooms. Sky villas are engineered with post-tensioned slabs to create expansive, column-free spaces, allowing for custom interior planning.
