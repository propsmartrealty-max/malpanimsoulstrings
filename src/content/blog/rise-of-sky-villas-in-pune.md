While often used interchangeably, there are distinct nuances between these two ultra-luxury configurations.

A Sky Villa or **duplex penthouse pune** usually spans two or more levels within a tower. It is architecturally designed to mimic an independent villa. The lower level often serves as the primary entertainment and living zone, while the upper level is reserved for private master suites.
